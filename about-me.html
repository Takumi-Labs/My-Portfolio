ABOUT ME

I'm Mariam Mudasiru, a Business graduate from the University of Ghana with an interest in the intersection of business, technology, and creativity. I enjoy learning how websites work from the ground up and turning designs and ideas into responsive, interactive experiences. My business background also influences the way I approach technology.

My journey into technology began through self-directed learning, including programmes such as CS50 and ALX, where I started developing my understanding of computer science and technology. Since then, I've been building my skills in web development, particularly HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

I'm interested not only in how something is built, but also in why it should be built, who it serves, and how it can create value. I'm currently continuing to develop my technical skills through hands-on projects and practical learning, with the long-term goal of combining my business knowledge with technology to solve real-world problems.
